# User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Questionnaire - UiTM Book e-Marketplace

This document contains a structured list of questions designed for a Google Form survey. Each item consists of a **Primary Question** (usually Yes/No or Rating) followed by a **Follow-up Question** to gather qualitative feedback.

---

## 🎨 Section 1: User Interface & Experience (UI/UX)

**Q1:** on a scale of 1 to 5, how would you rate the overall visual appeal of the "Glassmorphism" (frosted glass) design theme?
> **Follow-up:** logical continuation: "Did the design elements (transparency/blur) ever make the text difficult to read or buttons hard to find?"

**Q2:** Was the navigation menu (Home, Profile, Cart, etc.) easy to locate and use on your device?
> **Follow-up:** "Which specific page or menu item, if any, took you the longest to find?"

---

## 🔐 Section 2: Authentication (Login & Signup)

**Q3:** Were you able to successfully sign up using your UiTM Student/Staff ID?
> **Follow-up:** "Did the system clearly explain the difference between the Student (10-digit) and Staff (6-digit) ID requirements?"

**Q4:** Did the email verification process work smoothly for you?
> **Follow-up:** "If there was a delay, approximately how long did you have to wait for the verification email to arrive?"

---

## 🛍️ Section 3: Buying Experience

**Q5:** When searching for a book, were the search results accurate based on your keywords (Title, Author, or Subject Code)?
> **Follow-up:** "What specific search term did you use that perhaps didn’t give the results you expected?"

**Q6:** Did the "7-Day Warranty" and "Escrow Payment" badges make you feel safer about purchasing?
> **Follow-up:** "What additional information would you have liked to see to feel 100% confident in making a payment?"

**Q7:** Was the checkout flow (Cart -> Payment Simulation -> Receipt) easy to complete?
> **Follow-up:** "At any point during checkout, did you feel unsure about whether your payment was successful?"

---

## 🏷️ Section 4: Selling & Listing Management

**Q8:** Was the process of listing a new book for sale intuitive?
> **Follow-up:** "Which part of the 'List a Book' form took the most time or was most confusing to fill out?"

**Q9:** Did the system effectively notify you when someone was interested in your item?
> **Follow-up:** "Would you prefer to receive these notifications via email, or is the in-app notification bell sufficient?"

---

## 💬 Section 5: Negotiation System

**Q10:** Did you try using the "Make Offer" feature to negotiate a price?
> **Follow-up:** "If yes, did the chat interface update quickly enough for a smooth conversation with the seller?"

**Q11:** Was it clear when an offer was Accepted, Rejected, or Countered?
> **Follow-up:** "How could the system make the status of your offer more obvious on the screen?"

---

## 🐞 Section 6: System Reliability & Performance

**Q12:** Did you encounter any critical bugs or errors (e.g., pages not loading, buttons not working)?
> **Follow-up:** "Please describe the specific action you were taking when the error occurred."

**Q13:** How would you rate the loading speed of the application (especially images and dashboards)?
> **Follow-up:** "Which specific page felt the slowest to load during your session?"

---

## 📝 Final Feedback

**Q14:** On a scale of 1 to 10, how likely are you to recommend this platform to other UiTM students/staff?
> **Follow-up:** "What is the ONE major feature you would add to the system that is currently missing?"
